Favorite Movies

My top 3 movies of all time.

Howl's Moving Castle

A fantasy story about Sophie, a young hatter cursed into an old woman, who finds refuge inside a magical, walking mechanical fortress.

Her

A romantic science-fiction drama that follows a lonely writer who falls in love with an advanced artificial intelligence

Iron Lung

An independent sci-fi horror film about a convict who is forced to pilot a makeshift, rusting submarine through an alien ocean of blood on a desolate moon.
